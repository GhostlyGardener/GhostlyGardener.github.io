<i>I Am Reisen</i><br/>
An impressionistic music video inspired by Reisen, a character from Touhou Project, and the world of Gensokyo.<br/><br/>

Eientei keeps multiplying.<br/>
Doors, corridors, tatami rooms, the bamboo forest, and "myself" grow layer upon layer through endless time.<br/>
Some versions of me come from the past; others from futures that never happened. Some raise their guns at me; others lie beside me and say they envy my life.<br/>
But if every "Reisen" is equally real, how can I prove that I am the one who truly exists?<br/>
And so the dream eventually turns into cleaning.<br/>
Erasing the surplus worlds, other people's memories, and the other versions of myself, one by one.<br/>

---

<b>Background</b><br/>
The music was written several years ago around Reisen's character and the world and stories of Touhou Project. It draws on musical material from "Lunatic Eyes ~ Invisible Full Moon" and other Touhou themes, reworking it in several styles.<br/><br/>

Recently, the animation was completed with AI. Keyframes were planned by hand around the music's structure and story imagery, and H3-Max was used to generate the clips before editing.<br/>
