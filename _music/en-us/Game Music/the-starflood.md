<b>Overview</b><br/>
Countless meteors drag long traces of light across the night sky, like a slow tide passing over mountains, valleys, and open plains. Layered glows stretch from the horizon and fold distant shapes into moving color, as if the whole sky is slowly changing form. Interwoven trails continue to pass through view, building a vast and quiet scene between earth and sky. Even after the final streak disappears into the far night, the dreamlike afterimage lingers over the open world.<br/>
