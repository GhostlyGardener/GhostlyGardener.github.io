<b>Overview</b><br/>
I Said So is a web platform for recording public predictions before outcomes are known. Users submit a judgment on a verifiable public event, the record is locked with its original timestamp, and the platform later turns the result into a shareable proof card.<br/><br/>

The project is designed as a judgment-history archive. Its core product rule is that public predictions are immutable: users cannot edit, delete, or selectively hide records after submitting them.<br/>

<b>Core Features</b><br/>
- Event discovery with categories, tags, and ranked full-text search<br/>
- Append-only prediction submission with daily rate limits<br/>
- Prediction updates, comments, replies, likes, and notifications<br/>
- Public profiles with prediction history, hit rate, and sample size<br/>
- Shareable result pages for correct, incorrect, and unresolved predictions<br/>
- Admin tools for event publishing, moderation, submissions, feedback, and user management<br/>
